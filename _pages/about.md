MARKlab is a distributed cellular measurement platform developed at the **Chair of Communication Networks, University of Würzburg**, with research collaboration involving emnify.

The platform supports research on network performance, roaming, and energy consumption through remotely managed measurement nodes. It combines dedicated hardware with containerised experiments and remote SIM provisioning.
