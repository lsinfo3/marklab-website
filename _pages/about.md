MARKlab is a distributed cellular measurement platform developed at the **Chair of Communication Networks, University of Würzburg**, in collaboration with emnify.

We use remotely managed nodes to measure network performance, roaming, and energy consumption. Each node runs containerised experiments and can use SIM cards provisioned remotely.

## Collaboration with emnify

We work with emnify in the OMI and NaSA-OMI research projects. We develop and use MARKlab for measurements in these projects, studying cellular IoT connectivity, network performance, and device energy consumption.
