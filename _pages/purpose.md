## Understand cellular networks in their real operating conditions

Mobile network performance depends on more than a radio technology's specifications. Operator configuration, radio conditions, location, roaming paths, application behaviour, and device operation can all affect an experiment.

MARKlab provides a common measurement framework for investigating these factors in production networks. Dedicated nodes and centrally configured experiments help researchers compare observations while retaining information about their experimental conditions.

## Research directions

- **Quality of service:** latency, packet loss, throughput, and variability across networks.
- **Energy consumption:** current and voltage observations to investigate the cost of cellular IoT communication.
- **Multiple radio technologies:** experiments across supported legacy, LTE-based IoT, and 5G configurations.
- **Roaming and application behaviour:** measurements that connect network paths with the behaviour of Internet applications and protocols.
- **Measurement quality:** methods for evaluating whether observed results are consistent and capture the intended network property.

Energy measurements support energy-efficiency research. Broader sustainability conclusions require an explicit model and scope; electrical measurements alone do not describe a complete environmental footprint.
