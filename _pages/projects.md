## From OMI to NaSA-OMI

MARKlab is actively developed and used within collaborative research at the Chair of Communication Networks, University of Würzburg. The platform's development and experimental work span the completed **OMI** project and the current **NaSA-OMI** project.

These projects connect the development of a measurement platform with concrete research questions about cellular IoT networks. MARKlab provides the experimental environment for investigating network behaviour, performance, and device energy consumption.

## NaSA-OMI

<p><span class="status active">Current project</span></p>

**November 2025 – December 2028** · Partner: **emnify GmbH**

**Das Netz-als-Sensor: Analyse des Kernnetzes konvergenter Mobilfunksysteme zur Entwicklung neuer Geschäftsmodelle und optimierter Network Intelligence**

The network as a sensor: analysing the core network of convergent mobile communication systems to develop new business models and optimise network intelligence.

NaSA-OMI treats the network as a sensor: observations from cellular systems can help identify device and network problems and reveal opportunities to improve quality of service and energy consumption. The project investigates network intelligence for monitoring, analysis, and optimisation, alongside the integration and evaluation of non-terrestrial connectivity.

MARKlab continues to be developed and used in this project as a platform for experimental research.

**Funding:** Bayerisches Verbundforschungsprogramm (BayVFP) of the Free State of Bavaria, funding line “Digitalisierung”.

[Read the official NaSA-OMI project description](https://www.informatik.uni-wuerzburg.de/comnet/projects/nasaomi/).

## OMI

<p><span class="status planned">Completed project</span></p>

**October 2022 – September 2025** · Partner: **emnify GmbH**

**Optimierung und Modellierung eines IoT-zentrischen Mobilfunkkernnetzes**

Optimisation and modelling of an IoT-centric mobile core network.

OMI investigated the optimisation of a virtualised mobile core network for IoT. Its research covered device-specific traffic behaviour, network anomalies, quality assessment, and the energy efficiency of resource-constrained devices.

The project included building a testbed with real IoT devices and conducting measurements at the university and in emnify's core network. MARKlab was developed and used in this research context, providing a foundation for the experimental work continued in NaSA-OMI.

[Read the archived OMI project description](https://www.informatik.uni-wuerzburg.de/comnet/projects/archiv/next-generation-networks-projects/omi/).
