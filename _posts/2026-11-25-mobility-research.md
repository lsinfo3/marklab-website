The MARKlab mobility extension explores cellular measurements on the move. It investigates how movement and handovers affect latency, signal conditions, and energy consumption. The node remains under development.

The design adds a separate cellular management connection and battery-backed operation, reducing dependence on fixed Ethernet and power connections during experiments.

The research contribution **MARKlab Mobility Extension: Measuring the Impact of Mobility on Cellular IoT Connectivity** examines mobility effects in measured cellular IoT scenarios. It is included in the [30th Mobilkommunikationstagung programme](https://www.hs-osnabrueck.de/homepages/homepages-iui/fachtagung-mobilkommunikation/vortraege-der-30-mkt/).
