We are developing a mobile MARK node to measure how movement and handovers affect latency, signal conditions, and energy consumption.

The node uses a separate cellular connection for management and battery power, so experiments can run without fixed Ethernet or mains power.

The paper **MARKlab Mobility Extension: Measuring the Impact of Mobility on Cellular IoT Connectivity** describes this work and measurements in cellular IoT networks. It is listed in the [30th Mobilkommunikationstagung programme](https://www.hs-osnabrueck.de/homepages/homepages-iui/fachtagung-mobilkommunikation/vortraege-der-30-mkt/).

The mobility node is still under development. The [hosting guides]({{ '/host/' | relative_url }}) cover our stationary nodes. [Contact us]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) if you are interested in mobility research.
