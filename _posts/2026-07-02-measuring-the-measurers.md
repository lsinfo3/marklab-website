Our TMA 2026 paper examines how reliably MARKlab measures cellular network latency and whether the results reflect the network property we want to measure.

**Measuring the Measurers: An Evaluation of Reliability and Validity in Cellular Measurements** evaluates mean round-trip time (RTT) in a controlled LTE roaming setup. It checks the consistency of measurements and agreement between methods and reference measurements.

Different probing protocols can show similar trends but give different absolute latency values. Researchers therefore need to record the probing method and experimental conditions when comparing results.

See the [TMA 2026 paper listing](https://tma.ifip.org/2026/accepted-papers/) and [download the dataset from Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20327527).
