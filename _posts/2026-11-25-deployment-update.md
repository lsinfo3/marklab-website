MARKlab now has two measurement nodes in São Paulo, Brazil. Paris is also an active deployment, and the location inventory has been updated to reflect the platform's current footprint.

The updated inventory lists active locations across Europe and Brazil, including a node in Karlskrona, Sweden. Porto has been removed from the active deployment list.

Further deployments are planned in Warsaw, the United States, the Netherlands, Singapore, Australia, New Zealand, and Canada. Planned countries without a confirmed city appear in the inventory without a map pin.

These are deployment statuses, not live connectivity indicators. The two São Paulo nodes share a city marker; location counts should not be interpreted as total node counts.
