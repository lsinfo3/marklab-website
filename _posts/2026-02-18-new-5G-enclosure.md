We are working on a 5G version of the MARK node. We have chosen a modem and are adapting the enclosure to fit the extra antennas.

The new layout needs enough room for the antennas without making the case much larger. We are also checking the power supply and cooling for the 5G modem, particularly under load. We will test the layout with new 3D-printed enclosures.
