How much of a latency measurement reflects the network, and how much reflects the measurement method? MARKlab's TMA 2026 study examines reliability and validity in active cellular RTT measurements.

**Measuring the Measurers: An Evaluation of Reliability and Validity in Cellular Measurements** applies a structured assessment of measurement consistency, agreement, and validity. Its conclusions are tied to the tested LTE roaming configuration and mean RTT; they should not be generalised to every network metric.

For researchers planning a campaign, the practical message is to document the probing method and experimental conditions. Different protocols can follow related trends while producing different absolute latency values.

The [paper is listed at TMA 2026](https://tma.ifip.org/2026/accepted-papers/), and the [supporting dataset is available on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20327527).
